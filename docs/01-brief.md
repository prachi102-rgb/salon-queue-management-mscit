# Salon Queue — Project Brief & Scope

## Problem
Customers often wait without knowing their turn or expected waiting time. Reception staff must coordinate several barbers manually, which can cause confusion and missed turns.

## Users
- Customer: joins the queue and checks their token and wait.
- Receptionist: adds walk-ins, calls customers, and handles no-shows/cancellations.
- Barber: sees the assigned customer and marks service start/done.
- Owner: manages services, prices, staff availability, and queue opening.

## Must-have features
1. Mobile-first customer queue joining with name, 10-digit phone, services and barber preference.
2. Token generation, queue position and estimated wait.
3. Reception dashboard with walk-in, Call next, No-show and Cancel.
4. Barber view with Start, Done and break status.
5. Waiting-area display showing active barbers and next five tokens.
6. Owner settings for services, prices, barbers and online queue.
7. English/Gujarati language toggle.
8. Responsive charcoal-and-gold interface.

## Don't build
Online payments, future appointments, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, loyalty points, or multiple branches.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: charcoal (#1F2937), gold (#D4A017), rounded cards, readable mobile-first layouts.
