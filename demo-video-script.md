# 3-minute Demo Video Script

Record a phone screen or Chrome mobile viewport. Keep the app open and use sample data.

- 0:00–0:15 — Introduce Atelier Salon Queue Management System and show the Home screen.
- 0:15–0:55 — Tap Join Queue, enter a sample name and 10-digit number, select Haircut and Beard Trim, choose Any barber, and confirm.
- 0:55–1:20 — Show the generated token, position and estimated waiting time on My Status.
- 1:20–1:55 — Open Reception. Show the waiting list and tap Call next for an eligible barber.
- 1:55–2:25 — Open Barber View. Show the assigned customer, tap Start and then Done.
- 2:25–2:45 — Show the TV Display updating the current/next customer information.
- 2:45–3:00 — Open Owner Settings and briefly show queue open/close and service toggles.

Before recording, refresh the app and ensure the sample queue is in a clean state. Use a test phone number, not a real customer's personal number.
